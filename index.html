About

Science-driven biotechnology

Canaan Partners Inc. is a biotechnology company co-founded by Marine Ma and Dr. Janis Wong and based in Newport Beach, California.

Canaan Partners Inc. researches the most advanced biotechnology and carries it from the lab into real life, developing ingredients and finished products backed by clinical research. Our strength is our team of world-class biologists.

Dr. Janis Wong

Co-founder & Chief Scientist

A molecular biologist with more than 25 years of research experience and a PhD from the Chinese Academy of Sciences, Dr. Wong has dedicated her career to understanding the biological processes that drive health, aging, and skin function at the cellular level. She is a member of the Society of Cosmetic Chemists (SCC) and a Guest Professor at Tianjin University of Science & Technology.

Dr. JK Zhu

Advisor

Dr. Zhu is Vice-Chancellor and President of Macau University of Science and Technology, an internationally recognized scientist in gene editing and molecular genetics, and a member of the U.S. National Academy of Sciences. His research has made significant contributions to biotechnology and genetic science on a global scale.
