About

Patient capital for science-driven founders

Canaan Partners Inc. backs early-stage startups and gives young founders more than capital: guidance on financing, operator-level experience, and cross-border industry relationships.

Canaan Partners Inc. (CP) is the angel investment company of Marine Ma. We invest our own capital at the earliest stages in startups built on rigorous science — companies where the core insight comes from the lab, the dataset, or the model, and where the path to impact may be long but the upside for people is real.

Our mission is simple: help great scientists and technical founders get from first proof-of-concept to a durable company, with early capital, candid feedback, and thoughtful introductions.

Focus areas

Where we look

We are generalists about science and specialists about rigor. These are the areas we follow most closely.

Artificial Intelligence

AI applied to hard technical problems — scientific discovery, drug design, lab automation, and tools that make researchers faster. We favor teams with a defensible data or domain advantage.

Biotech

Therapeutics, diagnostics, and platform technologies grounded in strong biology. We look for a clear scientific hypothesis and a realistic plan for generating the data that matters.

siRNA & RNA Therapeutics

RNA interference and related modalities that silence disease-causing genes at the source. We are interested in delivery, targeting, durability, and new indications for this class of medicines.

Longevity & Anti-aging

Science aimed at extending healthy human lifespan — the underlying biology of aging, age-related disease, and measurement of biological age. We value careful evidence over hype.

Frontier Science

Other science-driven ventures that don’t fit neatly into a category — new materials, instruments, computation, and ideas early enough that the category doesn’t exist yet.

Approach

How we invest

  1. 01

    Early-stage

    We invest at the pre-seed and seed stages, often before there is revenue — sometimes before there is a product. We are comfortable with technical risk when the science is sound.

  2. 02

    Founder-first

    We back people. We look for scientific depth, intellectual honesty, and the resilience to build through setbacks, and we aim to be a helpful, low-friction partner.

  3. 03

    Science-driven

    We take the time to understand the underlying research. Our decisions start with the question: is the science real, and does it matter?
